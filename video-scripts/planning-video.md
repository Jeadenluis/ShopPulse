Hello everyone. My name is [Your Name], and I am a computer engineering student. For the Kilowott hackathon, I am planning a project called ShopPulse, a smart CRM for WooCommerce stores.

Here is the problem. Many small online store owners keep customer details in one place and orders in another. Because of that, they miss important moments: an order that is stuck as pending, a loyal customer who deserves a thank-you, or a past buyer who has not returned for months.

ShopPulse is planned to solve this by bringing customers and orders into one dashboard. A CRM helps a store owner understand every customer, and the WooCommerce integration means the data can come straight from the store instead of being typed in twice.

For the technology, I plan to use React for the interface, Node.js and Express for the backend, and MySQL for storage. The backend will talk to the WooCommerce REST API, and the API credentials will stay on the server, never in the browser.

The key features I plan to build are a dashboard with summary cards and a chart, customer management, order tracking, and a smart follow-up panel. The follow-up panel will use simple business rules, such as pending orders or no purchase in sixty days, and help the owner draft messages. It will not need a paid AI service.

If a live store is not available, I plan to include a clearly labeled demo mode.

I expect ShopPulse to save store owners time and help them keep customers coming back. Thank you.
